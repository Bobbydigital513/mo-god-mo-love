Her Story

Mo God. Mo Love. Mo Purpose.

Mo God Mo Love was born from my journey through pain, recovery, and purpose. I struggled with abandonment, depression, addiction, and feeling comfortable in my own skin.

Recovery gave me purpose and brought people into my life who love and believe in me. It taught me to look within and helped me find a God of my own understanding.

“As women, we often pour into others while forgetting the beauty, strength, and purpose God has placed within us.”

This brand is more than clothing. It is a movement of faith and inspiration expressed through style. I believe modesty can be fashionable, elegance can be powerful, and spirituality can be expressed through what we wear.

The name means a way of life: the more of God we embrace, the more love we can give — to ourselves, our families, our communities, and the world around us.

— Monique Lovelace, Founder

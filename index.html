My Story

I know what it feels like to not know my identity or my worth.

For much of my life, I never felt good enough. I felt like I was a mistake and that God had forgotten me. I battled abandonment, depression, addiction, and struggled with being comfortable in my own skin.

Recovery saved my life. It surrounds me with amazing people who love and believe in me. It’s teaching me to look within and has given me a God of my own understanding. Recovery created purpose in my life and helped shape the woman I am today.

Through that journey, I began discovering my identity, recognizing my worth, and understanding that the things I had been through didn’t have to define me—they could become part of my purpose.

And through my pain, my recovery, and my purpose, Mo God Mo Love was born.

“I created this brand to give other women the reminders I once needed myself: You are chosen. You are worthy. You are loved.”

Every piece carries a message of hope—not only for the woman wearing it, but for the woman who may see her and need that reminder too. Because sometimes you may be wearing the very message another woman needs to see.

Wear the message of hope. Be the reminder.

— Monique Lovelace, Founder

To Those Who Helped Me Become

Pieces of my story.

Mo God Mo Love carries pieces of my story—and the love of those who have inspired me along the way.

In loving memory of my mother, Cornelia Lovelace; my grandmother, Anna Lovelace; my grandfather, Memphis Lovelace; and my first Narcotics Anonymous sponsor, Gloria Northington. Though you are in heaven, your love and the lessons you shared continue to live in my heart.

To my son, A’veon Gilmore, my daughters, Arianah Lovelace and Amiah Lovelace, and my precious grandchildren: you inspire me to keep growing, keep believing, and build something rooted in love. You are part of the hope behind this brand.

To Narcotics Anonymous and my current sponsor Cynthia J.: thank you for giving me a place to recover, find connection, helping me discover my identity, and learn a new way to live clean. Recovery helps me discover that my story could become a source of hope for someone else.

To my friends and family: thank you for your love, encouragement, and belief in me. Your support has helped me keep moving forward.

And to all God’s children I encounter daily: every shared story, smile, and moment of connection reminds me why this brand exists. You inspire me to share hope and remind others that they are chosen, worthy, and loved.

Your place in my journey is woven into the heart of Mo God Mo Love.

With love and gratitude,Monique

Our Story

A word from Monique

Mo God Mo Love didn't start in a studio — it started in a season of holding on. Every piece we make carries a message I needed to hear myself: that I am not an accident, not a mistake, not defined by what I've walked through. I'm a product of God's grace, and so are you.

"I wanted a blazer that could preach without saying a word — something you could wear to church on Sunday and to a boardroom on Monday, and mean it either way."

Every blazer is made in small batches, sized S through 2X, in black — with more colors, more styles, and more of the story still to come.

— Monique M. Lovelace, Founder

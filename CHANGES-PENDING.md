(Her words, verbatim — supersedes the mission-statement version now on site.)

    I know what it feels like to not know my identity or my worth.

    For much of my life, I never felt good enough. I felt like I was a mistake
    and that God had forgotten me. I battled abandonment, depression,
    addiction, and struggled with being comfortable in my own skin.

    Recovery saved my life. It surrounds me with amazing people who love and
    believe in me. It's teaching me to look within and has given me a God of my
    own understanding. Recovery created purpose in my life and helped shape the
    woman I am today.

    Through that journey, I began discovering my identity, recognizing my
    worth, and understanding that the things I had been through didn't have to
    define me—they could become part of my purpose.

    And through my pain, my recovery, and my purpose, Mo God Mo Love was born.

    I created this brand to give other women the reminders I once needed
    myself:

    You are chosen. You are worthy. You are loved.

    Every piece carries a message of hope—not only for the woman wearing it,
    but for the woman who may see her and need that reminder too.

    Because sometimes you may be wearing the very message another woman needs
    to see.

    Wear the message of hope. Be the reminder.

    Mo God. Mo Love. Mo Identity.

## 3. A scripture tied to EACH blazer

### Product of God's Grace — 1 Corinthians 15:10 (KJV)
Section heading she gave: **THE MESSAGE YOU WEAR**
"More than a blazer. A powerful reflection of God's grace."

    "But by the grace of God I am what I am: and his grace which was bestowed
    upon me was not in vain; but I laboured more abundantly than they all: yet
    not I, but the grace of God which was with me."

Explainer: "Product of God's Grace" is a reminder that your past does not
define your worth. Through every struggle and every new beginning, His grace is
part of your story.

### I Am Fearfully and Wonderfully Made — Psalm 139:14 (KJV)
    "I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and wonderfully made: marvellous
    are thy works; and that my soul knoweth right well."

Explainer: "Fearfully" means with awe and reverence. This verse reminds you
that God made you with care and wonder—you are worthy of love, exactly as He
created you.
